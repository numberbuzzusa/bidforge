FAQ

Straight answers, before you ask

How fast can you turn work around?

Proposal drafts are delivered against the RFP deadline we agree on in writing — and a rush 48-hour option ($499) is available for tight solicitations. AI receptionist builds typically go live within one week of receiving your business details. Custom systems are scheduled per scope, with the delivery date fixed in your proposal.

For RFP proposals, who actually submits the bid?

You do — always. We research, pre-screen and write the complete draft, and give you a submission checklist. You review it, sign it, and submit it under your own business accounts. We never bid on your behalf and never hold your portal credentials.

What can the AI receptionist do — and what can it not do?

It answers questions from a knowledge base built on your real services, hours and pricing, captures lead details, and guides visitors through booking an appointment, 24/7. It does not give professional advice (medical, legal or otherwise), it does not access your internal systems unless we scope that together, and anything outside its knowledge base is captured as a lead for your team to follow up personally.

How do payments work?

We accept Payoneer and international wire transfer. Fixed-price work is invoiced per the written proposal — the quoted price is final, with no hourly add-ons. The receptionist monthly plan can be cancelled at any time.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Confidentiality is standard in our work — bid strategies, pricing and client information are never shared or reused. We are happy to sign your NDA before any detailed discussion, and we work under one whenever a client provides it.

Where are you based, and when can we reach you?

We deliver remotely and work with clients in the US and internationally, with daily overlap on US business hours for calls, reviews and deadlines. Most communication is handled by email and scheduled calls, so nothing depends on catching us by chance.
